Why this isn't the full picture

A calculator gives you a number. A dietitian gives you a plan.

This estimate uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, the same starting formula our dietitian uses — but it doesn't know your medical history, food preferences, or what's realistic for your week.

If you have diabetes, PCOD/PCOS, thyroid concerns, or are pregnant or breastfeeding, these numbers can be significantly off. Talk to a dietitian before using them to guide meal planning. No medicine or supplements are ever part of the plan.
